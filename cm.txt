fix: write N/A rather than a blank into a not-applicable follow-up

Okta marks "If yes, please describe" required and refuses to submit while
it is empty, even though the question above it was answered No. A blank is
logically complete but fails the employer's own validation, so the form
came back with a red "This field is required" on an application that had
nothing left to say.

"N/A" states only that nothing applies, which is what the negative parent
already said, so it asserts nothing new and satisfies the validator.
