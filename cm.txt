fix: tell a deliberate blank apart from a field nothing could fill

The drafting fix resolved Okta's "If yes, please describe" follow-ups, but
the submit guard still aborted the application: a resolved blank and an
unresolved one are the same empty string, so a finished form looked
incomplete.

Marks the answer instead of inferring it. notApplicable says blank is the
complete answer, and both guards - unresolvedRequired and the live fill
plan - honour it. A required field that is blank for no stated reason
still aborts, so the guard keeps its purpose.

Optional in the schema so undefined reads as false; stored packets drafted
before this change load unchanged.
