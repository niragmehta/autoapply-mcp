fix: leave a follow-up to a negative answer blank instead of blocking

Employers pair a yes/no question with a free-text follow-up that only
applies when the answer was yes:

  Do you have any outside business activities?  -> No
  If yes, please describe:                       -> nothing to describe

The follow-up carries no question of its own, so no profile answer could
ever match it and the whole application stalled waiting for a person to
type something that does not exist. Okta asked three of them on one form.

When the governing question was answered negatively, blank is the
complete and correct answer, so it now resolves rather than blocking.

Guards, because a wrong reading here silently drops a disclosure the
employer asked for:
- Only free-text fields. A select would need an option chosen on the
  candidate's behalf, which asserts something.
- Only a plainly negative parent. "No, but ..." does not count.
- The parent must itself be answered; a follow-up to an unresolved or
  human-blocked question stays blocked.
- Labels that merely begin with "if" and then ask a real question are
  untouched - Stripe's "If located in the US, in what city and state do
  you reside?" still needs a city.
