fix: take a lone consent option whatever the question's category

Roblox requires "Please review and acknowledge Roblox's Job Applicant
Privacy Notice" and offers exactly one option: a full sentence naming the
notice. Two applications blocked on it.

Two separate paths reached that field before the existing sole-consent rule
could apply. The label classifies as a legal attestation, so the category
gate returned first; and where a stored acknowledgement matched the question,
the approved-answer branch returned first because "Yes" is not the option
text. Both now apply the rule.

Consent is still never manufactured: sensitive categories are excluded, since
a lone "I agree" on a demographic or work-authorization question states a
fact rather than a formality, and a declining stored answer is never turned
into an agreement.
